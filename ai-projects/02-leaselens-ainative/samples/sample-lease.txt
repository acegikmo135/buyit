RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. Term. This lease begins on January 1 and runs for a term of 12 months. The lease renews automatically for successive one (1) year terms unless Tenant gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the term.

2. Rent. Monthly rent is $1,500, due on the first day of each month. Landlord may increase the rent at any time at its sole discretion.

3. Late Fees. A late fee of $200 plus $25 per day applies to any rent not received by the first of the month.

4. Security Deposit. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $4,500. The deposit is held by Landlord for the term of the lease.

5. Non-Refundable Fees. Tenant shall pay a non-refundable cleaning fee of $350 at signing.

6. Entry. Landlord or its agents may enter the apartment at any time without notice for inspection or any other purpose.

7. Repairs. Tenant is responsible for all repairs and maintenance to the unit, at Tenant's own cost.

8. Early Termination. If Tenant vacates before the end of the term, all remaining rent for the balance of the term becomes immediately due and payable.

9. Liability. Landlord shall not be liable for any injury, loss or damage to Tenant or guests, including damage caused by Landlord's negligence. Tenant agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Landlord.

10. Rules. Landlord reserves the right to change the rules, terms and conditions of this lease at any time.

11. Disputes. Tenant waives the right to a jury trial and agrees to binding arbitration of all disputes. Tenant shall pay Landlord's attorney fees in any dispute.

12. Pets. No pets are allowed without Landlord's written consent. Smoking is prohibited in the unit.
