RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. Term. The lease runs for a term of 12 months. After the term it continues month-to-month unless either party gives 30 days written notice.

2. Rent. Monthly rent is $1,200, due on the first day of each month. Rent will not be increased during the term.

3. Late Fees. If rent is received more than 5 days after the due date, a one-time late fee of $40 applies.

4. Security Deposit. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,200. Landlord will return the deposit, less lawful deductions for damage beyond normal wear and tear, within 21 days after move-out, with an itemized list. A move-out walk-through will be offered.

5. Entry. Landlord may enter the unit after giving at least 24 hours written notice, except in an emergency such as a fire or flood.

6. Repairs. Tenant should report needed repairs in writing or through the maintenance request portal. Landlord will repair heating, plumbing, electrical and structural problems within a reasonable time.

7. Early Termination. Tenant may end the lease early by giving 60 days written notice and paying a fee equal to one month of rent. Landlord will make reasonable efforts to re-rent the unit.

8. Disputes. In any legal action the prevailing party recovers reasonable attorney fees.

9. Pets. Cats and small dogs are allowed with a $200 refundable pet deposit.
